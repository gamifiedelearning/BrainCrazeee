About BrainCrazeee

BrainCrazeee is an educational gaming platform that helps learners practice, build confidence, and track their progress through interactive games and friendly competition.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

BrainCrazeee may collect account and learning information such as your username, email address, game scores, badges, achievements, and leaderboard rankings.

How we use information

We use this information to provide educational gaming services, save and display your progress, support multiplayer and leaderboard features, and improve your experience.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information or share it with unauthorized third parties. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate and support BrainCrazeee.

Account deletion requests

For account deletion requests, contact the BrainCrazeee Development Team through the designated support channel. Include your player name and registered email address to help verify your request. Removing app data does not automatically delete the separate sign-in account.

Terms & Conditions

  • Rankings, badges, or achievements obtained unfairly may be removed.
  • Accounts that violate these rules may be suspended or terminated.

BrainCrazeee reserves the right to modify platform features and policies. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated terms.

Contact Us
